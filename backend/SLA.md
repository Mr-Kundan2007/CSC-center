# CSC Center Service Level Agreement (SLA) Controls (Phase 13)

## Overview
Service SLA processing targets, response time thresholds, and operator escalation rules.

---

## 1. SLA Timeframes
- Default Application Review Target: 24 Hours
- Default Support Ticket Resolution Target: 12 Hours
- Document Re-submission Review Target: 12 Hours

---

## 2. SLA Pause & Resume Rules
- When an application status transitions to `document_required` or `payment_pending`, the active SLA timer is PAUSED.
- When customer uploads replacement document or completes payment, the SLA timer RESUMES.
